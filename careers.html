Sales and Marketing Representative Wanted

Are you confident in communicating with people, building relationships and identifying business opportunities? Dextsoft Systems invites a motivated individual to help us connect businesses with practical IT and digital services.

Position: Sales and Marketing Representative

Location: Ruai, Nairobi, Kenya

Work arrangement: To be agreed with the successful candidate

Payment: Commission-based; no fixed salary

About the Role

The successful candidate will promote Dextsoft Systems' services, find prospective clients and help turn enquiries into paying customers.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify and approach potential customers.
  • Promote website design and development services.
  • Market IT support and data analytics services.
  • Follow up on enquiries and potential sales.
  • Build positive relationships with clients.
  • Track leads and report sales progress.

Requirements

  • Good communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Confidence in engaging potential customers.
  • Self-motivation, honesty and professionalism.
  • Basic smartphone and computer skills.
  • Sales or marketing experience is an advantage.

Commission and Earnings

Payment will be based on an agreed percentage of money actually received by Dextsoft Systems from clients acquired through the representative's efforts. The commission rate, payment timing, how client leads are attributed, and any other applicable terms will be agreed in writing before work begins. Earnings depend on successful sales and collected client payments.
